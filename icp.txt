## Ideal Customer Profile

### Company Size
- 500–10,000 employees (mid-market to enterprise)
- Engineering org of 50+ developers

### Industries
- Financial services / Fintech
- Healthcare / MedTech
- E-commerce / Retail
- Insurance
- Travel & Hospitality
- Government / Public Sector (large contracts)

### Technology Signals
- Active web/mobile application development
- CI/CD pipeline in use (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, CircleCI, etc.)
- Selenium or Cypress already in their stack
- Agile/DevOps teams

### Business Signals
- Known pain: visual regression bugs reaching production
- High release velocity (weekly or more frequent deploys)
- QA team ratio: 1 QA per 5–10 devs
- Recent digital transformation initiative

### Negative Signals (disqualifiers)
- Less than 100 employees
- No active front-end application (backend-only)
- Budget < $50k/year
- No QA practice at all
