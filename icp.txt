# Applitools Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
# Used by the pre-meeting briefing AI to assess account fit.

## Core Question
What is the PRIMARY purpose of the customer's website or app?
- If it presents information, brand, or content → likely STRONG FIT
- If it is primarily transactional (e.g. e-commerce purchasing flow) → NOT our ICP
- If it is an internal enterprise platform → NOT our ICP

## Target Industries (Priority Order)
1. Financial Services & Banking — major banks, insurance, fintech, payment processors
2. Healthcare & Life Sciences — pharma, medical devices, health tech platforms
3. Media, Entertainment & Publishing — streaming, news/media, gaming, publishers
4. Technology & Software — large tech companies and specialized SaaS providers
5. Government & Public Sector
6. Education
7. Energy & Utilities
8. Telecommunications
9. Automotive & Transportation

## Target Personas (WHO we sell to)
- Developers and SDETs (Codefull): build and maintain automated tests in CI/CD pipelines
- Automation Engineers / Low-code/No-code test automation users (Codeless): design and manage large automation frameworks
- NOT: manual testers

## Strongest Fit Signals

### Content-Rich Websites (Codefull + Codeless)
Strong fit if the site has most of these:
- Rich visual content — many images, videos
- Dynamic content that changes frequently
- Multiple languages and locales
- Templates reused across multiple pages / brands / domains
- Brand consistency requirements across many properties
- CMS-driven (Adobe Experience Manager, WordPress, Drupal, Sitecore) → STRONG INDICATOR
- Some interactive elements (forms, flows) but NOT primarily transactional

Strong fit examples: media websites, corporate websites, brand websites, marketing sites, information portals

### Native Mobile Applications (Codefull)
Strong fit if:
- Consumer-facing brand or media app
- Financial services app
- Needs visual consistency across devices, OS versions, screen sizes

## ICP Examples (STRONG FIT)
- New York Times (nytimes.com) — content platform, templated, brand-consistent
- Chase Bank (chase.com) — financial services digital front door
- Johnson & Johnson (jnj.com) — healthcare brand/content site
- Condé Nast — media/publishing

## Outside ICP (Weak Fit / Disqualify)
- E-commerce focused sites where purchasing flows dominate (Nike, American Eagle)
- Single Page Applications that are NOT content-rich (e.g. eyes.applitools.com itself)
- Internal enterprise platforms: Workday, Oracle, SAP, Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics
- Primarily backend/API-driven systems with no meaningful front-end

## ICP Validation Data
- 67% of top healthy customers align with ICP
- Only 28% of churned customers fit ICP — misalignment is a leading churn predictor
- EMEA: low ICP alignment even in healthy customers — flag EMEA deals for extra scrutiny
- APAC: 100% healthy customer ICP alignment — strong signal, prioritize
- US: 90% healthy customer ICP alignment

## What We Test
- Visual Regression + Functional Assertions
- Accessibility Testing
- Component Testing (Storybook / Eyes)
- Native Mobile Testing
- Design Testing (design-to-code validation)
- NOT: Performance testing, end-to-end API testing, test management
